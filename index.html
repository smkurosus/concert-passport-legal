Privacy Policy — Setsy (Concert Passport)

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Setsy ("the app", "we", "us") is a concert-logging and social app that lets you track shows you've attended and connect with friends who share your concert history. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

What's collected depends on how you use the app:

  • Account identity. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier Apple provides (which may be a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email) and, optionally, your name if you share it during sign-in. If you sign in with email instead, we collect the email address you enter to send a one-time sign-in link.
  • Profile information you choose to add. A handle (username) and display name you pick after creating an account.
  • Content you create. Shows you log — artist, venue, city, date, genre, event type, and any notes or photos you attach — plus friend connections (requests sent, accepted, or declined).
  • Local, on-device data. Before you create a permanent account, the app works from a temporary anonymous session so you can start logging shows immediately. That data lives in local storage on your device and is only sent to our servers once you sign in with Apple or email.

We do not access your camera, photo library, contacts, or precise device location. Venue coordinates shown in the app come from a public venue/artist lookup, not from your device's GPS.

How we use information

  • To operate your account and sync your logged shows across sessions.
  • To show your shows and profile to friends you've connected with, and vice versa.
  • To let you find and add friends by handle.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with data brokers or ad networks. The app does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Third parties we use to run the app

  • Supabase — our backend provider. Account data, logged shows, and friend connections are stored in a Supabase-hosted database on our behalf, protected by row-level security so each account can only access its own data (and, for shows/profile, data explicitly shared via an accepted friend connection).
  • Deezer — a public music API we query by artist name to fetch artist photos for your logged shows. We only send the artist name; no account or personal information is sent to Deezer.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. This removes your profile, logged shows, and friend connections from our servers, and clears the data stored locally on your device.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Sign-in credentials and session tokens are stored using your device's secure keychain storage, not ordinary app storage. Server-side access controls (row-level security) ensure your account data is only readable by you and, where you've explicitly connected, your accepted friends.

Children's privacy

Setsy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
